Juice release notes

What changed in each version of Juice. Tap a version to open it. The app shows the latest three; everything earlier is here.

Latest versions

Version 4.0.020 Nov 2026Latest
  • New: Juice now speaks 11 languages. Pick yours on first launch, or change it any time in Settings
  • New first launch: the Juice film, a language choice, then a few quick questions
  • New 3D icon pack for Pro, and widget previews that follow your light or dark theme
  • Garmin: new Watch app and Setup guide cards in Settings
  • Juice Pro and Restore purchases now sit above the release notes; the PRO label and connected pill always stay lime
  • New setup guide, troubleshooting and privacy policy at sabrkei.com/portfolio/juice

Previous versions

Older releases, newest first.

Version 3.6.018 Nov 2026
  • Low battery: a small Low battery · charge soon pill on the device itself, instead of a banner
  • Fixed: phone battery capacity could read higher than when new
  • 7 new Pro icons: bulb, lock, remote, sensor, key fob, GPS and circuit earbuds
  • Removed Drops today from the connection card
  • Smoother and lighter on battery: widgets only redraw when something changes
Version 3.5.016 Nov 2026
  • Widgets: with several widgets, pick which one to preview and edit (heart rate included)
  • Widgets: new Add a new widget switcher for device or heart rate widgets
  • Phone card stats centred; app icons in battery use by app
  • Garmin: switched-off or removed watches no longer stay connected
  • Garmin Edge devices no longer listed twice or without a battery
  • New note on which devices can show a battery level
Version 3.4.215 Nov 2026
  • Fixed: earbuds showing as charging after being taken out of their case
Version 3.4.115 Nov 2026
  • Heart rate widget: choose accent colour, light or dark, and opacity. Tap it to open heart rate in Juice
  • New look for the heart rate section
  • My Devices: refresh button; one connected device gets a full-width card
  • Release notes: latest 3 in a scrolling card, older ones on sabrkei.com
  • Fixed: icon picker not scrolling; duplicate 3D icons removed
  • Fixed: battery % cut off in the widget picker preview
  • Charge cycles removed
Version 3.4.015 Nov 2026
  • Juice Pro: new heart rate widget for your Garmin watch, from 1×1 up to 4×4, with averages, today's low and high, resting heart rate and a trend line
  • The small heart rate line on the battery widget is still there for Pro
  • One battery widget: stretch it into a row, a column or a grid (widgets you already placed keep working)
  • My Devices: the connected count at the top is always green, and connection drop warnings are gone
  • Connection stability is no longer shown on device pages
  • Phone charge cycles no longer reset when Juice updates: Juice remembers the last count your phone reported and carries on from it
Version 3.3.014 Nov 2026
  • Juice Pro: your Garmin watch's heart rate in Juice and on your widget, with 5-minute and hourly averages and today's lowest, highest and average
  • Heart rate shows only when your watch reports it, updates about every 5 minutes, and stays on your phone
  • Update the Juice watch app from the Connect IQ Store to get heart rate, plus its new liquid battery look
  • Phone screen: see which apps used the most screen time since you unplugged, with an estimated share of battery used, and show or hide system apps (needs Usage access)
  • Fixed popups and buttons being cut off or unreachable when Font size or Display size is set very large
Version 3.2.114 Nov 2026
  • Garmin setup guide now opens the Juice page on sabrkei.com, with a link to the Juice watch app in the Connect IQ Store
Version 3.2.014 Nov 2026
  • Every widget style is now shown as a real preview, with a swipe hint, and Pro styles are visible before you buy
  • New Pro popup: 3D icons, everything Pro includes, one-time payment and free lifetime updates
  • Juice asks for Bluetooth access when you first open it, then shows an animated guide to Unrestricted battery
  • 3D icons keep their real colours by default, and Pro uses 3D icons for more device types
  • The app accent colour no longer changes widget previews, which now use green and red as standard
  • Edit a device's icon and name with the pen on its picture
  • Not connected devices say "last known battery level"; "Charge it soon" appears under connected devices
  • Phone charge cycles show the phone's own count, not Juice's
  • The app shows Juice Pro once you've bought it
Version 3.1.313 Nov 2026
  • Fixed: Get Juice Pro could say Google Play can't be reached until Juice was restarted
Version 3.1.113 Nov 2026
  • New Juice Pro screen: see everything Pro includes and unlock it with a one-time payment
  • Tapping any locked style, colour, icon or Garmin option now opens it
Version 3.1.013 Nov 2026
  • Juice Pro: one payment unlocks every widget style, colours, Garmin watches and 3D icons, and follows your Google account to new phones
  • Tapping a widget now opens Juice
  • 3D icons keep their own colours in the icon picker
  • The widget picker shows an up-to-date preview of your widget (Android 15+)
  • Garmin watches are found as soon as you open Juice, and any Garmin Connect problem is now shown in Settings
  • Updated for Android 16
Version 3.0.511 Nov 2026

Version 3.0.411 Nov 2026

  • Charge cycles now use the count the phone's battery has registered, when the phone shares it

Version 3.0.311 Nov 2026

Version 3.08 Nov 2026
