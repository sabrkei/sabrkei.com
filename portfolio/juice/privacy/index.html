Juice privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Applies to the Juice Android app (free and Juice Pro), the Juice watch app for Garmin, and the Juice pages on sabrkei.com.

In short: Juice does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything Juice reads is used on your phone to draw your battery, health and sleep widgets, and stays on your phone.

1. Who is responsible

Juice is published by Scott Abraham Keinström (developer name sabrkei). Because Juice does not collect personal data, there is no personal data held about you on our side. For any privacy question, contact contact@gbg-web.com.

3. Where data is stored and for how long

  • Everything is kept in Juice's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.
  • Battery and connection history is kept on your phone for seven days; older entries are removed automatically.
  • Health and Garmin readings are kept only as long as needed to draw the widgets and the short history they show (for example the last seven nights). You can switch heart rate off in Juice at any time.
  • Uninstalling Juice, or using Settings → Apps → Juice → Storage → Clear data, deletes all of it.
  • Juice has no servers and no network code of its own. It does not upload, sync or back up this data. (Android's own device backup may include app data depending on your phone's settings.)

4. Sharing

Juice does not share, sell, rent or transfer your data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or any purpose other than showing it to you. Health Connect and Garmin data is never used to train AI models or sent to any third party.

5. Health data

Juice's use and transfer of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect permissions policy and the Google Play User Data policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Juice is not a medical device. Heart rate, sleep scores, Body Battery and the suggested bedtime are informational estimates and are not medical advice or a diagnosis. Ask a health professional if you have health concerns.

6. Third-party services you may use with Juice

  • Google Play handles Juice Pro, a one-time purchase. Juice only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Pro. We never see or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase.
  • Garmin Connect and Connect IQ carry data from your watch to your phone. Garmin's privacy policy applies to what Garmin collects. The Juice watch app itself sends data only to the Juice app on your own phone.
  • Health Connect is Android's own health data store; you control which apps can read it.

7. Permissions Juice requests

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect and scan): read Bluetooth battery and connection. Never used to determine your location.
  • Usage access (optional): screen time by app.
  • Health Connect read permissions and background access (Pro, optional): the data listed in section 2, so widgets can refresh.
  • Foreground service (connected device) and receive boot completed: to keep widgets updating and restart after a reboot.
  • Battery optimisation exemption (optional): keeps widgets updating in the background.

Juice does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or messages.

8. Your rights and choices

Since Juice holds no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You are always in control on your device: withdraw any permission in Android settings or Health Connect, clear Juice's data, or uninstall it. If you are in the EEA, UK or another region with data protection rights, you keep all of those rights; contact contact@gbg-web.com and we will answer any request, including emails you have sent us (see section 10).

9. Children

Juice is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

10. Contacting support and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply and fix your problem. We keep support emails only as long as needed for that and do not add you to any mailing list. This website (sabrkei.com) does not use analytics or advertising cookies; it loads the Montserrat font from Google Fonts, which may let Google see your IP address, and stores your light or dark choice in your browser's local storage.

11. Security

Because data never leaves your phone, it is protected by Android's app sandbox and your device lock. Juice does not open any server connection to send your data. Keep your phone updated and locked.

12. Changes to this policy

If Juice changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships, and mention it in the release notes.
